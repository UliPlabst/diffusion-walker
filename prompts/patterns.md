Kaleidoscope: A pattern that is created by mirroring and rotating a single design element, resulting in a symmetrical and colorful pattern.

Tartan: A pattern of intersecting horizontal and vertical stripes that create a plaid design with different colored stripes crossing each other.

Mosaic: A pattern of small, irregularly shaped pieces of colored materials such as glass, ceramic or stone that are arranged in a repeating pattern to create an image or design.

Animal print: A pattern inspired by the markings or fur patterns of animals, such as leopard spots or zebra stripes.

Tie-dye: A pattern created by twisting and tying fabric in different ways before dyeing it, resulting in a unique and colorful pattern.

Camouflage: A pattern that imitates the colors and textures of its surroundings, often used in military clothing and outdoor gear.

Waves: A pattern of curving, wavy lines that resemble the movement of water or waves.

Digital: A pattern of pixelated, digital shapes and lines, often seen in computer graphics and modern designs.

Lace: A pattern of delicate and intricate designs, resembling traditional lacework or embroidery.

Rorschach: A pattern that resembles the famous Rorschach inkblot test, with symmetrical, mirrored designs that can be interpreted in various ways by the viewer.

Stripes: A pattern of evenly spaced, parallel lines of the same width, color, and intensity.

Polka dots: A pattern of small, circular dots that are evenly spaced and of the same size and color.

Herringbone: A pattern of zigzag lines that resemble the bones of a fish, with each line slanting in opposite directions.

Paisley: A pattern of curved, teardrop-shaped designs that resemble a twisted leaf or kidney.

Geometric: A pattern made up of geometric shapes such as squares, circles, triangles, and diamonds that are arranged in a repeating pattern.

Floral: A pattern of flowers and foliage, often with a repeating layout that creates a sense of harmony and balance.

Plaid: A pattern of intersecting lines, often with a criss-cross pattern of stripes in different colors.

Checkered: A pattern of squares that alternate between two colors, such as black and white.

Chevron: A pattern of zigzag lines that are arranged in a repeating V-shape pattern.

Abstract: A pattern that doesn't represent anything specific but is a combination of shapes, colors, and lines that create a unique and eye-catching design.
